Daily writing habit

Make writing feel small enough to keep.

A daily writing habit becomes easier when the goal is not a perfect page. The goal is a private return: one line, one thought, one feeling, or one memory saved before it disappears.

Daily writing prompts that stay light

What is one thing I noticed today?

Start with observation instead of pressure.

What feeling kept returning?

Let the entry be honest without needing a solution.

What helped, even a little?

Record the small supports that are easy to overlook.

What can wait until tomorrow?

Use the page to set something down gently.

Why short entries count

Short writing builds trust with the page. When a daily habit accepts ordinary entries, it becomes less fragile — and that makes it easier to keep returning over time.
