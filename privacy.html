How We Handle Your Information

What is stored

Journal entries are saved to your Google-linked cloud account when you sign in. If you use the site without signing in, entries, mood choices, custom emotion labels/images, and your optional PIN stay entirely in this browser using local storage.

What visitors can see

Other people who open the website do not see your entries. Their browser creates a separate journal space, and signed-in entries are separated by Google account. Your privacy is paramount.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in is used so your journal can follow you across devices. Your email is used solely to identify your account and securely sync your entries between your personal devices.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site may use Google AdSense to show ads to keep the journal free. Google and its partners use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings.

Cookie Policy

Quiet Journal Journey uses minimal cookies required for authentication (if you log in via Google) and basic site operation. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
