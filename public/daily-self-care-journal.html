A self care journal works best when it helps you notice what you actually need.

What to keep in a daily self care journal

How you feel

A mood check is often the simplest honest starting point.

What you need

Sometimes self care begins with naming rest, space, food, or support.

What helped

Writing down one useful habit or moment can make patterns easier to see.

What to carry forward

A gentle daily journal turns insight into something you can return to tomorrow.
