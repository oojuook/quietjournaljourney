What helps in a journal for stress

Private space

Stress writing works better when the page feels safe and unobserved.

Gentle prompts

A simple question can help anxious or overloaded thoughts become more specific.

Mood awareness

Tracking how you feel makes patterns easier to notice over time.

Minimal design

Less visual noise makes it easier to focus on what actually feels heavy.
