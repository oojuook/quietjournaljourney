Self reflection journal

Reflection becomes easier when the page stays gentle.

A self reflection journal can help you notice patterns, name what mattered, and end the day with more understanding than judgment. The goal is not to analyze everything. It is to stay close to what feels true.

Questions that suit a self reflection journal

What felt meaningful today?

Start with one moment, not the whole day.

What part of me needed more care?

Reflection becomes more useful when it includes compassion.

What kept returning?

Repeated feelings and thoughts often tell you what needs attention.

What do I want to carry forward?

End with one insight, one value, or one small next step.
