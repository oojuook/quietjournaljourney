Journal for overthinking

When thoughts are loud, a journal can help them slow down.

A journal for overthinking does not need to solve everything. It can simply turn a spinning thought into words, a fear into a sentence, or a long spiral into something you can look at with more space around it.

Helpful ways to journal through overthinking

Name the loudest thought

Write the thought exactly as it is before trying to fix or reframe it.

Separate facts from fear

Ask what actually happened, what you are imagining, and what you still do not know.

Write a kinder version

Offer yourself one calmer sentence that is still honest, just less punishing.

Leave one next step

End with something small and real so the page does not keep looping after you close it.
