It should feel calm enough to think in

A journal app should support reflection with clear spacing, gentle structure, and less visual noise around the writing itself.

It should support both short and deep writing

Some days you only need a sentence. Other days need more room. A good journal app should let both kinds of pages feel natural.

It should help you revisit yourself

Looking back matters. Mood markers, saved entries, and clean navigation can make reflection more meaningful over time.
