# Quiet Journal Journey SEO Plan

- Product: private online diary and mood journal
- Brand: Quiet Journal Journey
- Audience: people looking for calm digital journaling, mood tracking, reflection prompts, and privacy

### Supporting intent

- homepage copy aligned to core keyword targets

## Next practical steps
1. Set one permanent production domain
2. Add the domain to Google Search Console
3. Submit sitemap.xml in Search Console
4. Request indexing for the homepage
5. Publish one dedicated landing page for each major keyword cluster
6. Publish at least 4 to 8 supporting articles
7. Build backlinks from journaling, wellness, and productivity sites

## Search Console checklist
- Verify the final production domain
- Submit: /sitemap.xml
- Inspect the homepage URL and request indexing
- Monitor queries and impressions weekly
- Track click-through rate for homepage title and description

## Content guidance
- One main keyword intent per page
- Use the brand name consistently
- Keep H1 focused and clear
- Add internal links from articles to core landing pages
- Use natural language, not keyword stuffing

## Ranking expectation
- Broad terms like "diary" or "journal" are highly competitive
- Better early targets are long-tail searches such as:
  - private online diary
  - mood journal
  - online diary with password
  - aesthetic digital journal
  - reflection journal app
