How to use a private online diary well

  • Keep the first step small. One line, one feeling, or one win from the day is enough.
  • Write for clarity rather than perfection. The goal is to notice your inner world, not produce polished entries.
  • Use prompts on harder days so the page feels like support instead of pressure.
  • Review older entries occasionally to notice emotional patterns and shifts in energy.
